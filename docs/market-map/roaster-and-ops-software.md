# Roaster & ops software

As of 2026-10-19. Part of the [Specialty Coffee Software Market Map](specialty-coffee-software.md).

An independent roastery's software stack has four layers: roast logging, production and inventory, selling, and the cafe. Cropster is the only vendor in all four. Every other vendor covers one or two, so most small roasters combine a free roast logger, a production tool and Shopify.

## 1. Roast logging and profiling

Most tools charge by roast volume or batch count. Free open-source tools (Artisan, Typica) are the default for small and home roasters, and more roaster makers now bundle their own software.

| Tool | Who it's for | Price (as listed) | What stands out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| [Firescope](https://eng.firescope.cropster.com/pricing) (owned by Cropster since Oct 2025) | Small roasters, strongest in Korea, Japan and Asia | Free (10 roasts/month); Starter 26 €/month; Cropster Core+ from 95 €/month | 3,000+ small roasters; cloud records; green inventory deducted from batch size |

## 2. Production, inventory and wholesale

This is where small roasters feel the most pain: turning orders into roast days, tracking green and roasted stock, and serving wholesale cafes.

| Tool | Who it's for | Price (as listed) | What stands out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [RoasterTools](https://roastertools.com/pricing) | Independent roasters, from under 1,000 lb/month to 120,000 lb/month | Essentials $99/month (1,000 lb included) → Organize $199 → Automate $499 → Scale $999 → RT Plus $2,300; per-lb fee above the allowance | Coffee-specific: green stock deducted as you roast, wholesale ordering portal, production planning, QuickBooks sync, unlimited users |
| [Cropster Commerce](https://cropster.com/pricing/commerce) (part of Cropster Scale) | Roasters with wholesale and e-commerce | Included in volume-priced plans; add-ons: sample management 199 €, contracts and costs 500 €, API 100 € | Pulls orders from Shopify, WooCommerce and Beans into roast plans; first-in-first-out stock allocation; traceability from green bean to bag |
| [Unleashed](https://www.unleashedsoftware.com/industry/coffee-roasters-inventory-management/best-coffee-roasting-software/) | Larger, multi-site roasters | From $399/month (3 users) | General inventory system: batch records, reordering, multiple locations |
| Katana | Growing manufacturers | $179–$799/month, billed annually (per Unleashed's page) | General manufacturing software with batch and expiry tracking |
| Craftybase | Micro-batch makers | $30–$90/month (per Unleashed's page) | Batch costing and material usage |

## 4. Cafe operations

Cafes run on general point-of-sale systems; there is little coffee-specific software.

| Tool | Who it's for | Price (as listed) | What stands out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Cropster Café](https://www.cropster.com/packages/) | Multi-site cafes, roaster-owned cafes | From 39 €/month per espresso machine | Machine data: shot counts, waste, consistency, maintenance alerts |
| General POS (Square, Toast, Lightspeed) | Every cafe | Varies | Sales and staff; no link back to the roastery or green coffee |

## What this means

- **Cropster is consolidating the roastery market.** After Verdane's 2024 stake and the Firescope deal, expect more acquisitions. Independents' main alternative is RoasterTools plus a free logger.
- **Pricing scales with pounds roasted,** so costs climb with volume (RoasterTools from $99; Cropster volume fees after 250 kg).
- **The handoff from sourcing to the roastery is manual.** artisan.plus importing green purchases from three importers shows the demand. No tool connects price context, supplier relationships and cuppings to the roast plan.
- **Open questions:** current Cropster Roast monthly prices (its price calculator isn't public), Bottomless's terms for roasters, and RoastLog's v5 tier prices confirmed by the vendor.
